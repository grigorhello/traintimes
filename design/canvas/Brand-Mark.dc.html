Train Times · mark and app icon

A train head, nothing else. Cab, windscreen, two lights, the rail under it. White on brand green; reads as a train at 29 px.
App icon. White train head on brand green #0C7B53. iOS applies the mask.
Dark icon. Mint #6EE7B7 on #0B1F17. Tinted icon: same shapes, white on black; iOS applies the colour.
Contained symbol. In-app, watchOS, macOS and print; takes the current text colour.
Geometry Cab 64 × 62 units with 18-unit top corners and 10-unit bottom corners; windscreen 44 × 22, lights 12 in diameter, rail 52 × 7. Three white shapes on one flat field, no gradients, no shadow, no outline. Never: letters in the icon (a white G or GO on green is GO Transit's mark), GO's olive green, GO or Metrolinx logos or livery, status colours on the mark, a clock or badge added to it.
Size ladder · iOS icon at 120, 76, 60, 40 and 29 px
In-app glyph: the symbol as a 22 pt mask, for Settings › About and anywhere the app refers to itself.
