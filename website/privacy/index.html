Privacy

Train Times has no account and no login. It shows no ads and contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

On your iPhone

Your saved route, favourite stations and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us.

Train alerts

If you turn on an alert, the app sends our alert service an anonymous installation id, an Apple push notification token, your 12 or 24-hour time setting and the alert rule (the train, stations and days). This is used only to send you delay and cancellation notifications. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID.

How long it is kept

  • An alert rule is deleted 24 hours after its last service date, or when you delete it in the app.
  • The push token and installation id are deleted when you delete the app, or after 30 days without use.

We do not sell or share this data. Apple delivers the notifications.
