Privacy

Train Times has no account and no login. It shows no ads and contains no advertising trackers.

On your iPhone

Your saved route, recent routes and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us.

To show train times, the app downloads GO Transit's published schedule from Metrolinx about once a week. Nothing about you is sent with the download.

Your location

Train Times works without your location. If you allow it, the app uses it on your iPhone only: when you open the app, to show trains from the station you’re closer to (Settings › Automatic direction turns this off); while the app is open, to ask if you are on a GO train; and during a ride you start, to show the minutes to your stop and tell you before it. If you turn on “Detect my train”, which needs location “Always”, the app also checks for a GO train while it is closed. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else, and the app keeps no history of where you were. It keeps only what follows from a reading: which way your route points, and a ride in progress. Our analytics service sees a rough area from your internet address, such as a city. It is not your iPhone’s location.

Train alerts

If you turn on an alert, the app sends our alert service an anonymous installation id, an Apple push notification token, your 12 or 24-hour time setting and the alert rule (the train, stations and days). This is used only to send you delay and cancellation notifications. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID.

How the app is used

To learn which features help riders and what to improve, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics service: for example that a route was set (its two GO stations), a train was opened or an alert was turned on, with the app version, iOS version and iPhone model. Each install gets a random id that is not linked to you, to your alerts or to any other company's data. No name, email, location or anything you type is sent, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking. Usage events are kept for one year.

How long it is kept

  • An alert rule is deleted 24 hours after its last service date, or when you delete it in the app.
  • The push token and installation id are deleted when you delete the app, or after 30 days without use.

We do not sell or share this data. Apple delivers the notifications.
